The challenge

Great engineering knowledge
should travel further.

Turn civil and environmental engineering assets into knowledge products that are verifiable, reusable, and built for long-term stewardship.

Datasets, models, workflows, code, and educational resources carry valuable engineering insight. Your challenge is to make that insight understandable, reviewable, and useful beyond the project that created it.

Bring a real engineering problem. Demonstrate a concrete mechanism for trust. Show how your idea could connect contributors, reviewers, practitioners, and AI-enabled tools across communities.

Make trust demonstrable

Show how your knowledge earns confidence.

Strong projects make these five qualities visible to both human reviewers and AI-enabled systems.

Provenance

Where did it come from, and who created it?

Integrity

How do we know it has not changed?

Interpretability

What does it mean, and where does it apply?

Reusability

Can another community use it with context intact?

Governance

Who reviews, maintains, and takes responsibility?
