## 1. The real engineering problem

What is the civil or environmental engineering context? Who needs this knowledge, and what goes wrong when the underlying asset is difficult to interpret, verify, review, or reuse?

## 2. The asset

Identify the dataset, model, workflow, code, simulation, educational resource, or other asset. Describe its source, creator, version, relevant assumptions, permissions, and current limitations.

## 3. The proposed knowledge product

Explain your mechanism. What have you structured, encoded, connected, verified, or governed? Who contributes, reviews, challenges, maintains, and uses it? Link to the artifact and explain how to inspect or run it.

## 4. Trust and reuse

- Provenance: Where does the asset come from? Who is accountable for it?
- Integrity: How can reviewers detect alteration or misrepresentation?
- Interpretability: What does it mean, when does it apply, and where should it not be used?
- Reusability: How does another project or organization use it without losing context?
- Governance: Who reviews and maintains it? How can a user challenge or correct it?
- Human review: What happens before an engineer or AI-enabled system relies on it?
