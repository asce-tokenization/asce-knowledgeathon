ASCE ENGINEERING KNOWLEDGE-A-THON
Submission preparation checklist

Final schedule and rules will be confirmed by the organizing committee.
Contact: asce.tokenization@gmail.com

TEAM AND SCOPE
[ ] Choose one primary challenge pathway; other pathways may also inform the work.
[ ] Identify the civil or environmental engineering domain and real problem.
[ ] Confirm one designated team lead and at least one ASCE member on the team.

REQUIRED MATERIALS
[ ] Concise project brief.
[ ] Concrete core artifact or prototype with clear review instructions.
[ ] Trust and reuse explanation: provenance, integrity, applicability, limitations, human review, reuse.
[ ] Ecosystem growth statement connecting beyond the immediate project.
[ ] Practical demonstration video no longer than 10 minutes; slides alone are insufficient.
[ ] AI-use disclosure with tools, purposes, affected work, validation, and limitations.
[ ] Licensing and ownership note with permissions, attribution, and reuse restrictions.

LINKS AND ACCESS
[ ] Organize files in a dedicated GitHub repository or Google Drive folder.
[ ] Include a README or index mapping all submission components.
[ ] Provide a YouTube or Google Drive link to the demonstration video.
[ ] Verify all links and video playback in a signed-out/private browser.
[ ] Ensure reviewers have view access to every required component.
[ ] Contact the committee in advance if private review access is needed.
[ ] Remove confidential, unrelated, or unauthorized material from the shared folder.
[ ] Identify a stable release, commit, or version where practical.

FINAL CHECK
[ ] Review the published rules and confirmed deadline on the event website.
[ ] Submit the required links through the event submission form.
[ ] Keep a copy of the links and submitted information.

This checklist is a preparation tool. Completing it does not submit your project.
