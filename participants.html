Engineering Knowledge-a-Thon

Participants

A shared challenge for people who understand engineering, build digital tools, and care about trustworthy knowledge.

Bring your perspective.

Participation is open to a broad range of academic, professional, and disciplinary backgrounds. Teams that combine engineering context with technical and organizational expertise are encouraged.

Undergraduate & graduate students
Engineers & industry practitioners
Faculty & researchers
Public-sector & community contributors

Relevant expertise includes civil and environmental engineering, data science, AI, software, standards, infrastructure systems, policy, education, and governance.

Build a team that can deliver.

  • One person or a teamTeams may have one or more members. No formal maximum is specified in the current draft guidelines.
  • At least one ASCE memberEach team must include at least one ASCE member.
  • One designated team leadThe lead coordinates communication and submission. An individual may lead only one team.
  • Clear roles and meaningful contributionsCross-level, cross-domain, and cross-institutional teams are welcome.

Final eligibility details and any participation restrictions will be confirmed before competition launch.

No team yet? Start here.

  1. Introduce your interests

    Use registration to tell the committee your engineering domain, skills, and whether you are looking for teammates.

  2. Describe the help you need

    A domain expert might look for a developer. A builder might look for someone who understands review and reuse in practice.

  3. Confirm your team

    Select a lead, identify your primary pathway, and check that your team includes an ASCE member.
