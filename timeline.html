Engineering Knowledge-a-Thon

Timeline

From an initial idea to a reviewable knowledge product. Plan around these upcoming milestones.

Dates to be announced. This is the planned milestone sequence. The committee will publish confirmed dates, deadlines, time zones, and workshop arrangements here.
  1. Date TBAMilestone 1

    Individual registration

    Sign up and indicate whether you are seeking teammates.

  2. Date TBAMilestone 2

    Challenge briefing webinar

    Explore the purpose, pathways, submission expectations, evaluation criteria, and example directions.

  3. Date TBAMilestone 3

    Team registration

    Confirm your team lead, engineering domain, ASCE membership, and primary pathway.

  4. Date TBAMilestone 4

    Mid-term check-in / Q&A

    Ask questions and clarify scope before finalizing your project.

  5. Date TBAMilestone 5

    Final submission

    Provide the complete required material and a practical demonstration video through accessible links.

  6. Date TBAMilestone 6

    Phase I: review and evaluation

    Reviewers assess submissions against the announced criteria. Finalist arrangements will be confirmed.

  7. Date TBAMilestone 7

    Phase II: finalist workshop

    A final workshop with presentations and demonstrations is planned. Date, venue, format, and finalist count remain subject to confirmation.

  8. Date TBAMilestone 8

    Recognition and awards

    Recognize selected projects and explore showcase or follow-up opportunities.
