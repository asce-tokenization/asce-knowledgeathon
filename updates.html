Awards · Proposed structure

Recognition across all three pathways.

The proposed award pool totals $6,000. Each pathway would recognize one $1,200 winner and either two $400 honorable mentions or one $800 honorable mention / nomination.

Final award arrangements will be confirmed before competition launch.

Participant guidance

Make the demonstration practical.

Every submission should include a video of no more than 10 minutes showing the artifact, prototype, schema, workflow, or governance process in use. A slide-only presentation does not satisfy the demonstration requirement.
