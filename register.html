Tell us how you would like to contribute to the engineering knowledge ecosystem.

Join the Knowledge-a-Thon community.

Register your interest as an individual or with a team. The registration form helps the committee coordinate participation, share event information, and support team formation.

Complete the registration form

Use the event's Google Form to provide your participation and team information. The form opens in a new tab.

Open registration form ↗

Questions? asce.tokenization@gmail.com

Have this information ready.

  1. Your participation details

    Name, contact email, affiliation, background, and engineering interests.

  2. Your team status

    Let the committee know whether you already have a team, are participating individually, or are looking for teammates.

  3. Your proposed direction

    Select a primary pathway and describe your interests. Teams should designate a lead and identify at least one ASCE member.

What happens next?

The committee will use registration information for event coordination and participation updates. Follow the updates and timeline pages for confirmed milestones and any next steps.

Registration information does not automatically appear in the public participant directory. Public profiles should be shared only with participant permission.
